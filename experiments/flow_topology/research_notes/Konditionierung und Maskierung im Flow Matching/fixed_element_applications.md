# Applications of diffusion / flow matching that generate permutation-invariant sets or graphs with some elements fixed

Scope note: emphasis on mechanisms that transfer to a set transformer generating block-corner coordinates (unordered set, OT coupling) plus edge-curve tokens, where some corners / curves are known exactly. Peer-review status marked per source where identifiable.

## Protein motif scaffolding with flow matching / diffusion (FrameFlow, RFdiffusion, Genie2, FrameDiff, MultiFlow, Proteina)

### Takeaway
The field has converged on *amortized* conditioning — train the generator with the known elements present as clean, unnoised inputs plus a binary "is-known" mask, with motifs synthetically sampled from unconditional training structures — and this beats inference-time conditioning (guidance, replacement/inpainting) by roughly an order of magnitude in strict success rate. Translation invariance is handled by zero-centering the *known* part together with the noise so the model cannot memorize absolute offsets; the number of free (scaffold) elements is simply sampled from a user/benchmark-specified range, not predicted by the model.

### Cited Findings
- FrameFlow (SE(3) flow matching) offers two motif-scaffolding routes: **motif amortization** ("FrameFlow is trained with the motif as input using a data augmentation strategy") and **motif guidance** ("performs scaffolding using an estimate of the conditional score from FrameFlow without additional training") — [arXiv:2401.04082 (TMLR 2024, peer-reviewed)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04082)
- FrameFlow amortization feeds the motif as clean, fixed frames plus "a 1D mask as additional input with a 1 at the location of the motif and 0 elsewhere" — [FrameFlow HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.04082v2)
- FrameFlow zero-centers the motif and the initial noise sample ("within the zero center of mass (CoM) subspace") explicitly to prevent the model from memorizing scaffold locations from the motif's offset to the origin — [FrameFlow HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.04082v2)
- FrameFlow's motif data augmentation creates pseudo-labels from ordinary PDB structures: random residues as segment starts, segments extended, motif fraction sampled in [γ_min=0.05, γ_max=0.5]; each protein is reused across epochs with a freshly sampled motif — [FrameFlow HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.04082v2)
- FrameFlow motif guidance is a Bayes-rule decomposition ∇log p_t(T|T^M) = ∇log p_t(T) + ∇log p_t(T^M|T), approximated through the network's denoised prediction, i.e. no retraining — [FrameFlow HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.04082v2)
- FrameFlow results on the 24-motif benchmark: FrameFlow-amortization 20/24 solved with **353 unique designable scaffolds** at 18 s/sample, vs RFdiffusion 20/24 with 141 at 50 s, and TDS (twisted diffusion sampler, guidance-type) 19/24 with 217 at 117 s; summarized as "2.5 times more designable and unique motif-scaffolds compared to state-of-the-art" — [FrameFlow HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.04082v2); [abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04082)
- Head-to-head on the RFdiffusion motif benchmark (11 targets x 100 samples), conditioning strategy matters far more than model class: **amortised 20.0 %**, **reconstruction guidance 1.5 %**, **replacement 0.5 %** success; success = pAE < 5 AND scRMSD < 2 Å AND motifRMSD < 1 Å AND pLDDT > 70 AND scTM > 0.5 — [Didi et al., arXiv:2312.09236 (NeurIPS 2023 AI4D3 workshop)](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.09236v1)
- Same table, decomposed: scRMSD < 2 Å — amortised 35.1 %, reconstruction guidance 45.1 %, replacement 0.6 %; motifRMSD < 1 Å — amortised 50.0 %, guidance 4.2 %, replacement 24.3 %. Guidance produces designable but motif-infidel structures; replacement keeps the motif but destroys designability — [Didi et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.09236v1)
- Didi et al. give the taxonomy used across this literature: replacement method, reconstruction guidance, amortised/conditional training, and Doob's h-transform (theoretically exact conditioning, complex to implement) — [arXiv:2312.09236](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.09236v1)
- Genie 2 encodes motifs **invariantly**: motif residues are marked by one-hot vectors concatenated to the single-residue features, and motif geometry enters as the **pairwise Cα distance matrix** of the motif — so inter-motif geometry is left unspecified and the model "learns to satisfy the constraints of each motif while generating self-consistent configurations of inter-motif geometries" — [Genie 2, arXiv:2405.15489 (preprint, May 2024)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15489v1)
- Genie 2 training-time motif tasks are synthetic: 1–4 motif segments per protein covering 5–50 % of residues; scaffold segment lengths are sampled at inference to meet the problem's constraints — [Genie 2 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15489v1)
- Genie 2 trains on ~588,570 AFDB structures (pLDDT > 80, length ≤ 256), the first protein diffusion model to use AFDB — [Genie 2 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15489v1)
- Genie 2 single-motif: 22/24 tasks solved; multi-motif: 4/6 tasks, including a three-epitope immunogen — [Genie 2 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15489v1); reported per-problem unique successes out of 1,000 samples: 3BIK+3BP5 17, 1PRW_four 11, 1PRW_two 8, 4JHW+5WN9 4, 2B5I 0, 3NTN 0, and benchmark success rates 44.5 % (active sites), 53.4 % (binding), 71.2 % (structural features), average 56.4 % — [search-derived from Genie 2 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.15489) (numbers came via search snippet, not verified in the fetched HTML — treat the per-category rates as needing a check against the PDF tables)
- MotifBench (2025) was created because "significant variability in evaluation strategies across publications has hindered comparability of results"; it fixes a pipeline, metrics and 30 problems, with a reference implementation — [MotifBench, arXiv:2502.12479 (preprint)](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12479v1)
- Cross-method totals reported alongside MotifBench-style evaluation: Proteina 2,094 total successes > Genie 2 1,445 > FrameFlow 1,099 > RFdiffusion 889; on the Design24 set FrameFlow had the most designable scaffolds in 13/24 motifs vs Genie 2 7/24 and RFdiffusion 6/24 — [search-derived from MotifBench/Proteina PDFs](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.12479) (snippet-level; the split between the two benchmarks should be confirmed in the source tables)
- Proteina (flow matching, scaled) does motif scaffolding by **fine-tuning the unconditional base model** with motif conditioning (Appendix B) and states state-of-the-art motif-scaffolding performance; lengths 32–256 residues in main training, fine-tuned up to 768 and sampled up to 800 — [Proteina, arXiv:2503.00710 (preprint; references ICLR)](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.00710v1)
- MultiFlow (multimodal flow matching, joint structure+sequence co-design) achieves state-of-the-art co-design and lets "the same multimodal model be used for flexible generation of the sequence or structure", i.e. one trained model serves several conditioning patterns — [arXiv:2402.04997, ICML 2024 (peer-reviewed)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04997)

### Inferences
- The amortized-vs-guidance gap (20 % vs 1.5 %) is the single most transferable result: for block corners/edge curves, training the set transformer *with* known corners present (clean, masked) should be strongly preferred over post-hoc guidance or RePaint-style replacement on an unconditional model.
- Genie 2's invariant motif encoding (intra-motif pairwise distances only) is the right pattern when the *relative pose* between known groups is not fixed; for block corners where the known corners' absolute coordinates are exact and shared with the free corners, the FrameFlow pattern (clean coordinates + binary mask + joint zero-centering) is the closer analogue.
- Across all of these, the count of free elements is an *input* (sampled scaffold lengths, specified total length), not something the generator infers — suggesting a separate count model or user-specified count for free corners.

### Gaps
- MultiFlow's exact conditioning mechanism (mask channels, which modality is clamped, any OT coupling between noise and data) could not be extracted from the abstract page; the full paper/appendix was not fetched.
- Proteina's Appendix B motif-conditioning details (mask representation, whether motif coordinates are fed clean) and its exact MotifBench table were not retrieved.
- Per-problem Genie 2 / Proteina numbers obtained only from search snippets; not verified against the PDFs.

## Molecule generation with fixed fragments / linkers (DiffLinker, DiffSBDD, scaffold-constrained generation)

### Takeaway
Equivariant molecular diffusion handles fixed atoms in two canonical ways: (a) *conditional* models that concatenate the fixed context into the same graph but never noise it and zero the predicted displacement on those nodes, and (b) *inpainting* on an unconditional joint model, where fixed nodes are replaced each step by their forward-noised ground truth, which needs RePaint-style resampling and a center-of-mass re-alignment to work at all. The number of free atoms is drawn from a separately trained size predictor or an empirical size distribution.

### Cited Findings
- DiffLinker builds "a single fully-connected graph where nodes are represented by coordinates and feature vectors" containing both noisy linker atoms and the fixed context; coordinate displacements are **zero for context nodes**, so the context stays frozen across all denoising steps; node features carry atom types, timestep and fragment flags — [DiffLinker (ar5iv HTML of arXiv:2210.05274)](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.05274)
- Translation invariance in DiffLinker: data is centered at the **fragment center of mass** before noise is sampled, and the predicted noise is corrected as ε̂ = EGNN(z_t, u, t) − [z_t^r, 0] — [DiffLinker ar5iv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.05274)
- DiffLinker's linker size comes from a **separately trained GNN classifier** ("Size GNN") over the categorical distribution of linker sizes seen in training, taking a fully connected fragment graph with atom types and inter-atomic distances as input; the user may override it — [DiffLinker ar5iv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.05274); checkpoints for DiffLinker, "DiffLinker (given anchors)" and Size GNN are released per dataset (ZINC, GEOM, pockets) — [official repo](https://github.com/igashov/DiffLinker)
- DiffLinker also has anchor-free and anchor-conditioned variants: default models need no attachment points, separate "given anchors" checkpoints condition on user-specified anchors — [official repo](https://github.com/igashov/DiffLinker)
- DiffLinker numbers: ZINC validity 93.8 %, recovery 82.0 %, SC-RDKit ≥ 0.9 in 67.85 %; GEOM multi-fragment validity 94.0 %, recovery 86.8 %, novelty 68.6 %, SC-RDKit ≥ 0.9 73.56 % vs 3DLinker 8.22 %; pocket-conditioned generation averages 7 steric clashes vs 6 in reference complexes and 21 without pocket conditioning — [DiffLinker ar5iv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.05274)
- DiffLinker is peer-reviewed: Igashov, Stärk, Vignac et al., "Equivariant 3D-conditional diffusion model for molecular linker design", *Nature Machine Intelligence* 2024 — [repo citation](https://github.com/igashov/DiffLinker)
- DiffSBDD-cond: the ligand point cloud z_t^(L) is supplemented with protein-pocket nodes "that remain unchanged throughout the reverse diffusion process"; both are processed as distance-based graphs — [DiffSBDD HTML (arXiv:2210.13695v3; published in Nature Computational Science 2024)](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.13695v3)
- DiffSBDD-joint uses inpainting at inference on a model trained on the joint distribution: at every step "we replace the generated atoms corresponding to fixed nodes with their forward noised counterparts" — [DiffSBDD HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.13695v3)
- Naive replacement introduces inconsistencies between fixed and generated regions; DiffSBDD adopts **iterative resampling (RePaint)** where "each latent representation is repeatedly diffused back and forth before advancing to the next time step", which the paper reports as essential for molecular connectivity — [DiffSBDD HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.13695v3)
- Equivariance fix for inpainting: "we translate the fixed atom representation so that its center of mass coincides with the predicted representation" at each step — [DiffSBDD HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.13695v3)
- DiffSBDD atom counts: sampled from the smoothed empirical joint distribution p(N_L | N_P) (ligand size given pocket size), or fixed manually — [DiffSBDD HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.13695v3)
- DiffSBDD inpainting results by fraction of fixed atoms: scaffold hopping (27.3 % fixed) mean Vina −7.6 ± 2.5 kcal/mol vs −5.69 reference; scaffold elaboration (72.7 % fixed) −8.10; fragment linking (73.4 % fixed) −7.74 ± 1.8, "performing comparably to specialist model DiffLinker"; validity ~80–99 % — [DiffSBDD HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.13695v3)
- The DiffSBDD implementation lets the user name reference-ligand atoms to fix and optionally "sample additional atoms near the center of mass of the fixed substructure", i.e. known elements also initialize the free ones — [DiffSBDD repo mirror](https://github.com/Yoik/DiffSBDD)
- The overall claim is that one pretrained diffusion model plus inpainting covers scaffold hopping/elaboration and fragment growing/merging "without the need to retrain new models" — [DiffSBDD, Nature Computational Science version (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11659159)

### Inferences
- The two mechanisms map directly onto the block-corner problem: "clean known corners as extra tokens with a flag + zero the velocity on them" (DiffLinker-style, amortized) vs "replace known-corner tokens with their noised ground truth each step + resample" (DiffSBDD-joint-style, training-free). The protein numbers above argue for the former; DiffSBDD's experience argues that if the latter is used, resampling and re-centering are mandatory.
- Both domains anchor the coordinate frame on the *fixed* part (fragment CoM / zero-CoM of motif+noise). For block corners with exactly-known corners, centering on the known corners is the analogous choice and removes the translation nuisance without breaking the known values.
- The "size GNN" pattern (a small separate classifier over the count of free elements, conditioned on the known elements) is the most directly reusable recipe for predicting how many free corners / edge curves to generate.

### Gaps
- Flow-matching-specific molecular models with fixed fragments (EquiFM, and other flow-matching molecule generators) were not covered by the searches performed here; whether they use OT coupling together with fixed atoms is unresolved.

## Point-cloud completion and layout generation with fixed elements (PVD, LayoutDM, LayoutDiffusion, LayoutFlow)

### Takeaway
Point-cloud completion uses exactly the "partial observation held clean, loss only on the free points" recipe; layout generation (the closest analogue to a token set with attributes) shows quantitatively that *training-time* masked conditioning dominates inference-time trajectory manipulation, and it handles variable element counts by padding to a maximum with padding tokens rather than predicting a count.

### Cited Findings
- PVD factorizes the shape as x_0 = (z_0, x̃_0) with z_0 the partial observation and x̃_0 the free points; "the partial shape is fixed at z_0 for all time" in the conditional forward process, the network sees partial points plus the noisy free points, and "only the subset x̃_{t−1} is used for L² loss" — [PVD (ar5iv HTML of arXiv:2104.03670), ICCV 2021 (peer-reviewed)](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2104.03670)
- PVD counts: 1,024 points given as partial input, 2,048 points total generated; completion on the GenRe benchmark CD ×10³ = 0.4415 (airplane), 3.211 (chair), 1.774 (car); multimodal completion on PartNet TMD 1.91/1.70/5.92 and MMD ×10³ 1.27/1.03/1.98 for chair/table/lamp — [PVD ar5iv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2104.03670)
- PVD frames the same model for "unconditional shape generation and conditional, multi-modal shape completion", i.e. one model, conditioning by clamping the known subset — [ICCV 2021 paper page](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/html/Zhou_3D_Shape_Generation_and_Completion_Through_Point-Voxel_Diffusion_ICCV_2021_paper.html)
- LayoutFlow (flow matching for layouts) conditions by **marking the conditioning input inside the element embedding** while distinguishing four conditioning scenarios (e.g. type known, type+size known, completion, refinement); masks are randomly sampled with equal probability over tasks during training — [LayoutFlow HTML (arXiv:2403.18187), ECCV 2024 (peer-reviewed)](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.18187v2)
- LayoutFlow explicitly reports that training-based conditioning "significantly outperforms conditioning during inference" (trajectory-modification/replacement style), with far better FID — [LayoutFlow HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.18187v2)
- LayoutFlow handles variable element counts by padding to N_max ("we pad the remaining dimensions"); each element is a continuous vector of 4 geometry values plus B "Analog Bits" for the categorical type, processed by a Transformer encoder that is order-agnostic — [LayoutFlow HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.18187v2)
- LayoutFlow completion task (20 % of elements given) FID 1.51 (RICO) / 1.10 (PubLayNet) vs LayoutDM 6.80 / 25.02; refinement FID 0.77 / 0.18 vs LayoutDiffusion 0.55 / 2.05, with far fewer inference steps — [LayoutFlow HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.18187v2)
- LayoutFlow's single model covers "generating from scratch, completing a partial layout, and refining an existing layout" through its conditioning scheme — [ECCV 2024 page](https://ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/html/5246_ECCV_2024_paper.php)

### Inferences
- LayoutFlow is the closest published precedent for the target architecture: unordered attribute-carrying tokens, flow matching, several conditioning patterns (including "some elements fully known") learned by random mask sampling in one model. Its "mark the condition inside the element embedding" is the recommended mechanism for known corners/curves, and its training-vs-inference-conditioning ablation is the strongest non-protein evidence for amortization.
- Notably, LayoutFlow reaches SOTA without OT coupling or explicit permutation matching — padding + a permutation-equivariant Transformer and plain (independent-coupling) flow matching sufficed. This is a data point that OT coupling is an optimization, not a requirement, for set flows.

### Gaps
- LayoutDM's and LayoutDiffusion's own conditioning internals (LayoutDM uses discrete diffusion with masked/"unknown" tokens; LayoutDiffusion uses a per-attribute corruption schedule) were not fetched directly here, only their numbers as reported in LayoutFlow's tables.
- No source found that performs layout/point-set flow matching with an explicit OT/Hungarian coupling restricted to the free elements.

## CAD / B-rep and mesh generation with partial given parts (BrepGen, MeshGPT)

### Takeaway
B-rep diffusion does partial-input autocomplete by RePaint-style replacement of a subset of face tokens with the given geometry diffused to the current time step, releasing the clamp in the last ~50 steps so the known parts can settle; cardinality is handled by duplicating tokens up to a fixed maximum and de-duplicating geometrically afterwards, which doubles as the mechanism for recovering topology.

### Cited Findings
- BrepGen's CAD autocomplete is inpainting "inspired by RePaint": during face denoising "a random subset of face tokens are replaced with the provided face geometry diffused to that time step"; the replacement **stops in the final 50 denoising steps** to allow minor adjustments, then edges and vertices are generated to complete the solid — [BrepGen HTML (arXiv:2401.15563), ACM TOG / SIGGRAPH 2024 (peer-reviewed)](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.15563v3)
- BrepGen represents a solid as a hierarchical tree (solid → faces → edges → vertices) and denoises node features level by level with Transformer diffusion; duplicated nodes encode topology and are **detected and merged** at inference ("nodes with similar features are detected and merged across different parents to recover mating and adjacency relations, and within each parent to restore unique geometric elements") — [BrepGen HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.15563v3)
- BrepGen does not predict cardinality: it uses "a predetermined maximum branching factor for each tree level and randomly duplicate[s] nodes until reaching the maximum number of children" (30–50 faces; 30–40 edges per face depending on dataset), then de-duplicates with geometric thresholds (0.08 Euclidean for bounding boxes, 0.2 for decoded points) — [BrepGen HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.15563v3)
- BrepGen reports COV / MMD / JSD distribution metrics plus Novel / Unique / Valid (watertight) percentages, and lists CAD autocomplete and design interpolation as applications; it is the first such model to include free-form and doubly curved surfaces — [BrepGen HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.15563v3); [BrepGen PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15563)
- MeshGPT (autoregressive over quantized triangle-embedding tokens) performs shape completion by conditioning on a partial token prefix and "can infer multiple possible completions for a given partial shape"; it reports +9 % shape coverage and ~30-point FID improvement over prior mesh generators, at 30–90 s per mesh — [MeshGPT, CVPR 2024 (peer-reviewed)](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Siddiqui_MeshGPT_Generating_Triangle_Meshes_with_Decoder-Only_Transformers_CVPR_2024_paper.html); [arXiv:2311.15475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.15475)

### Inferences
- BrepGen's "clamp the known tokens, then release the clamp for the last steps" is a cheap, directly portable trick for exactly-known corners/curves if a replacement scheme is used at all: it lets the free elements finish consistently while keeping the known ones effectively exact.
- BrepGen's duplicate-then-merge padding is an alternative to count prediction for unordered geometric sets: generate a fixed-size padded set and collapse near-duplicates. For block corners this is attractive because duplicate corners are geometrically detectable, and the same mechanism recovers which blocks share a corner (topology) — close in spirit to collapsing corner tokens into shared block vertices.
- Autoregressive mesh/CAD models (MeshGPT, SolidGen-style) get partial-input conditioning for free via prefixes, but at the cost of imposing an element ordering — the exact property the target set-transformer design avoids.

### Gaps
- SolidGen's partial/autocomplete behaviour was not verified from a primary source in this session (no fetch performed).
- No quantitative autocomplete-specific numbers (e.g. completion fidelity to the given faces) were found for BrepGen; its metrics are reported for unconditional generation.

## Permutation symmetry combined with fixed elements: OT coupling, matching, and counting free elements

### Takeaway
No source was found that explicitly excludes fixed tokens from the OT/permutation coupling — but two results bracket the problem: permutation-invariant OT requires minimizing the coupling cost over the symmetry group (otherwise the minibatch OT map is hopeless for symmetric targets), and naive minibatch OT in *conditional* flow matching provably skews the prior conditional on the condition, which must be corrected by putting the condition into the cost matrix. Together they imply: match only over the free elements, and make the coupling cost condition-aware.

### Cited Findings
- Equivariant flow matching (Klein, Krämer, Noé, **NeurIPS 2023**, peer-reviewed) shows that for highly symmetric densities "accurately approximating the optimal transport (OT) map may require a prohibitively large batch size", and that this is most acute for permutations, since "even for small system sizes, it is unlikely for any pair of target-prior samples to share the exact same permutation"; the fix is a cost function that **minimizes over group elements** (rotations and permutations) inside the OT flow-matching algorithm, giving shorter paths and better scalability — [arXiv:2306.15030](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.15030)
- C²OT ("The Curse of Conditions", **ICCV 2025**, peer-reviewed) shows that "the default optimal transport mapping disregards conditions, resulting in a conditionally skewed prior distribution during training", creating a train/test mismatch because inference draws from the unbiased prior; the fix is "a conditional weighting term in the cost matrix when computing the optimal transport assignment", validated on 8gaussians→moons, CIFAR-10, ImageNet-32 and ImageNet-256 with discrete and continuous conditions — [arXiv:2503.10636](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10636); [ICCV 2025 paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Cheng_The_Curse_of_Conditions_Analyzing_and_Improving_Optimal_Transport_for_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf)
- A related line notes that in restoration/inpainting tasks the degraded–target pairs "naturally form optimal transport couplings", i.e. the known part itself largely determines the coupling — [search summary over flow-matching/OT papers incl. arXiv:2503.10636](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.10636) (snippet-level, not verified in a primary table)
- Count handling observed across the surveyed systems: scaffold/segment lengths sampled from a specified range (Genie 2, FrameFlow) — [Genie 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15489v1); a separately trained size classifier (DiffLinker Size GNN) — [DiffLinker](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.05274); an empirical conditional size distribution p(N_L|N_P) (DiffSBDD) — [DiffSBDD](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.13695v3); padding to N_max with padding tokens (LayoutFlow) — [LayoutFlow](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.18187v2); duplication-to-max followed by geometric de-duplication (BrepGen) — [BrepGen](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.15563v3)
- In the set-prediction literature, permutation-equivariant set generators avoid imposing an ordering, and TSPN (Transformer Set Prediction Network) "outperforms DSPN in the quality of predicted set elements and in the accuracy of their predicted sizes", i.e. set size is an explicitly predicted quantity; a generative set model must satisfy exchangeability and handle variable cardinality — [Conditional Set Generation with Transformers, arXiv:2006.16841 (ICML 2020 workshop-era preprint)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.16841); [SetVAE, CVPR 2021](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/papers/Kim_SetVAE_Learning_Hierarchical_Composition_for_Generative_Modeling_of_Set-Structured_Data_CVPR_2021_paper.pdf)

### Inferences
- Combining the two OT results gives a concrete prescription for the target model: compute the minibatch/within-sample OT assignment **only over the free corners** (the known corners are not free to be permuted — their identity is given by the condition), and include the known elements in the cost (C²OT's condition-aware weighting) so the induced prior over free tokens is not skewed by the conditioning pattern. Doing a symmetric-group-minimized cost over *all* tokens, known included, would be the error C²OT describes.
- Because known corners pin the frame, the usual OT-over-rotations/translations nuisance minimization (Klein et al.) can be partly replaced by alignment to the known part (FrameFlow/DiffLinker centering) — cheaper and exact.
- No surveyed system predicts the count jointly with the set in a permutation-invariant generator; all of them either take the count as input, sample it from a prior, or pad-and-prune. Pad-and-prune (BrepGen-style, with geometric de-duplication) and a small conditional count classifier (DiffLinker-style, conditioned on the known corners/curves) are the two realistic options.

### Gaps
- I found **no paper** that explicitly states "fixed/known tokens are excluded from the OT coupling and only free elements are matched". This appears to be an unaddressed combination in the published literature as of the searches performed (Oct 2026); the recommendation above is an inference from Klein et al. + C²OT, not a cited result.
- No source found that quantifies the benefit of OT coupling specifically for *conditional set* generation with exactly-known elements (LayoutFlow, the closest system, does not use OT coupling at all).
- Whether C²OT's weighting has been applied to set/graph-valued conditions (as opposed to class labels and images) is unknown.
